# Know Your Genio ✨

> *"Software designed not just to be used, but to be wished into existence."*

A calm, living companion living in your macOS menu bar. Type a wish, and a living, responsive interface shapes itself on demand — with 100% on-device privacy.

---

## ⚡ 1-Click Awakening (Terminal)

Open **Terminal** (`Cmd + Space` &rarr; `Terminal`) and paste:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abhi32dev/Genio-Releases/main/scripts/awake.sh | bash
```

**What this does automatically:**
1. ✦ Cleans any previous running build
2. ◆ Installs the fresh release universal binary into `/Applications/Genio.app`
3. ◈ Configures environment & PATH (`$HOME/.local/bin`)
4. ❖ Connects AI companion (Claude / Codex)
5. ✧ **Tada! Ready.** Opens Genio right in your macOS menu bar!

## 🧭 Canvas Navigation & Controls

| Action | Control / Gesture | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Multi-Screen Floating** | Always On Top | Floats over any application, fullscreen space, or Mission Control desktop without space-switching |
| **Move Anywhere** | Click & Drag Header | Drag from the header bar (`GENIO ✨`) to place the window anywhere on your display |

Click the Genio icon in your menu bar, type into **"Make a Wish..."**, and press Return:

### ☀️ Morning Inspiration

### 📊 Living Instruments & Telemetry
